Origin & Philosophy

Solid Kiss SLU (Andorra) originated in 2022 by rebranding the Latvian company Yagni IK. The renaming reflects a strategic evolution in architectural philosophy. While the predecessor was conceived as a lean, short-term entity named after the software principle YAGNI ("You Aren't Gonna Need It"), the transition to Solid Kiss demonstrates a permanent commitment to long-term software design maturity, combining the structural integrity of SOLID design principles with the pragmatic efficiency of the KISS ("Keep It Simple, Stupid") methodology.

Work Style & Philosophy

Software development is an optimization problem. Every process, architecture, and decision should ultimately be evaluated by a single metric: efficiency.

Direct and transparent communication is the most effective form of collaboration. Clear feedback, constructive disagreement, and open discussion produce better decisions than consensus driven by hierarchy or politics.

The best ideas should win regardless of their source. Strong engineering cultures minimize ego and maximize objective decision-making, allowing technical merit to take precedence over individual ownership.

Team success is the only success that matters. Removing blockers, sharing knowledge, and helping teammates are not acts of individual contribution—they are investments in the team's ability to deliver value.

Individual performance should be measured primarily by its impact on the team's outcomes. External competition between teammates creates local optimization; shared ownership creates sustainable, high-performing engineering organizations.
